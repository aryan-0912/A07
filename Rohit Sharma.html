Rohit Sharma

Rohit Sharma is an Indian international cricketer and the former captain of the Indian national cricket team. He is regarded as one of the most successful opening batsmen in modern cricket.

Rohit is known for his elegant batting style, powerful stroke play and ability to score big hundreds. He is the only player to have scored three double centuries in One Day International cricket.

Rohit Sharma has represented India in all three formats of the game. He has also been a successful captain and has led India in several major international tournaments.

Career

Early career

Rohit Sharma made his international debut for India in 2007. He initially played as a middle-order batsman before becoming one of India's leading opening batsmen.

His performances in domestic cricket and the Indian Premier League helped establish him as an important player. He became known for his ability to build long innings and score quickly.

Rise as an opener

Rohit's career changed significantly after he began opening the batting for India in One Day International cricket. He went on to score several memorable centuries and became one of the most dangerous opening batsmen in the world.

In 2014, he scored 264 runs in an ODI against Sri Lanka, which became the highest individual score in ODI cricket at that time.

Captaincy

Leadership

Rohit Sharma became an important leader for India and also achieved great success as captain of the Mumbai Indians in the Indian Premier League.

Under his leadership, Mumbai Indians won multiple IPL titles. Rohit later became India's captain across different formats.

Major achievements

Rohit has scored centuries in all three formats of international cricket. He has also represented India in multiple World Cups and has been one of the team's most important batsmen.

Playing style

Rohit is famous for his timing, pull shot, effortless six-hitting ability and calm batting style. He is particularly effective once he settles at the crease and is capable of converting starts into very large scores.
